Screen Copilot

Ask live questions about whatever is on your screen.

Clicking Launch opens a small tab. From there, click "Start Watching" and choose a screen or window to share. A floating window will pop out and stay on top of your other apps.

Each provider's key is stored only in this browser (chrome.storage.local) and is sent directly to that provider's API — never to any third-party server. Switching providers here doesn't erase the other keys, so you can flip back and forth.
